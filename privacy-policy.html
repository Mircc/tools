Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-06-23

Thank you for using the tools on this website. We take your privacy seriously, and privacy protection is a core design principle of this site.

1. We Collect No Data

All tools on this website are purely front-end implementations that run locally in your browser. No backend server is involved.

We do not collect anything you type, including text, URLs, Wi-Fi passwords, location data, or any other input. No registration is required to use any feature.

  • We do not collect anything you type.
  • We do not store any user data. There is no database, no log files, no server-side records.
  • We do not transmit your input to any remote server. Your data never leaves your device.

2. All Processing Happens in Your Browser

With the exception of the Secure Message key rotation mechanism — which must be hosted on Cloudflare Workers for security reasons — every computation, conversion, and generation operation runs entirely inside your browser. When you close the page or tab, any related data is cleared from memory.

3. We Do Not Use Tracking Cookies

This site does not use cookies for user tracking or behavioral analytics. If minor functional preferences (such as your chosen interface language) need to be retained, they are saved only in your device's local localStorage, solely for that specific function, and never used for tracking or analytics.

4. No Analytics SDK

This site does not integrate Google Analytics, Baidu Analytics, 51.la, or any other third-party analytics SDK.

5. Third-Party Services

Some features may make calls to the following public, anonymous third-party APIs to retrieve necessary information:

FeatureThird-Party ServiceWhat Is Sent
IP address display api.ipify.org Your public IP (carried by your network request itself)
IP geolocation ipapi.co Your public IP

These API requests are only triggered when you actively use the relevant feature. The information retrieved is used solely for on-page display and is not retained by this website. Please refer to the respective privacy policies of those third-party services.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via email at privacy@tuku.eu.org.

This Privacy Policy applies to all tool pages on this website.
